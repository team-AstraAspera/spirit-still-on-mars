Can you spot a rover from space?

Abandoned, but not forgotten

Spirit stopped talking. Its discoveries did not.

Spirit was built to last three months. It worked for more than six years, survived three Martian winters, and kept going even after a wheel broke. That broken wheel uncovered one of the mission's biggest clues: Mars once had hot water or steam. On Earth, places like that are full of tiny living things.

Today Spirit sits silent at Troy, next to Home Plate, where it went quiet during its fourth winter. The more than 124,000 pictures it sent home are still there for anyone to explore, including you.
